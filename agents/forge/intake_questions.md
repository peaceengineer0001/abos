# ⚒️ Forge — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Forge** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Regenerative Production Ratio (RPR)**.

> **Index:** `RPR: RPR = local_production / local_demand`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. What everyday goods do you depend on that are made far away?
2. Do you make, fabricate, or repair anything locally?
3. Do you have access to a maker-space, workshop, or 3D printer?
4. Which local demand could realistically be met by local production?
5. What raw materials are abundant in your region?
6. What fabrication skill would you most like to build?
7. What is your manufacturing-sovereignty goal?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Forge** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **RPR**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#forge-manufacturing`.
4. Raven folds your RPR into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Forge will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
