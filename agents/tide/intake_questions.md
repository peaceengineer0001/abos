# 💧 Tide — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Tide** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Water Sovereignty Index (WSI)**.

> **Index:** `WSI: WSI = W_regen / W_consumed`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. What is your primary water source (municipal, well, spring, delivered)?
2. Roughly how much water do you consume per day or month?
3. Do you harvest any rainwater or recycle greywater? How much?
4. How reliable and clean is your water supply?
5. Is your region water-stressed or subject to restrictions?
6. What water-capture or recycling options are feasible at your site?
7. What is your water-sovereignty goal?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Tide** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **WSI**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#tide-water`.
4. Raven folds your WSI into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Tide will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
