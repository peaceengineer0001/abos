# 🌱 Root — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Root** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Local Nutrition Ratio (LNR)**.

> **Index:** `LNR: LNR = Food_local / Food_total`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. Roughly what fraction of your food is grown or produced locally?
2. Do you grow, raise, forage, or fish any of your own food? How much?
3. How much growing space or land do you have access to?
4. How do you preserve or store food for off-season?
5. Which traditional or ancestral foods are part of your diet?
6. How exposed are you to food-supply disruptions?
7. What is your food-sovereignty goal for the coming year?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Root** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **LNR**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#root-food`.
4. Raven folds your LNR into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Root will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
