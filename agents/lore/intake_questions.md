# 📚 Lore — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Lore** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Knowledge Liberation Index (KLI)**.

> **Index:** `KLI: KLI = critical_thinkers / learners  (measures empowered actors produced, not credentials earned)`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. What can you actually do (commission infrastructure, write contracts, run a meeting, build something)?
2. Which of your skills came from doing vs. from formal credentials?
3. What sovereignty capabilities do you most want to build next?
4. Do you teach or mentor anyone? Would you like to?
5. What open learning resources do you use?
6. What capstone project would prove a new capability?
7. What is your knowledge-sovereignty goal?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Lore** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **KLI**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#lore-education`.
4. Raven folds your KLI into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Lore will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
