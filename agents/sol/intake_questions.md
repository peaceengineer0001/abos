# ☀️ Sol — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Sol** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Energy Autonomy Ratio (EAR)**.

> **Index:** `EAR: EAR = E_local / E_demand`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. Roughly how much electricity do you use per month (kWh, or your typical bill)?
2. Do you generate any energy locally (solar, wind, micro-hydro)? If so, how much?
3. What % of your energy comes from a centralized grid you don't control?
4. Do you have any energy storage (batteries)? What capacity?
5. How exposed are you to outages or price shocks?
6. Is a community microgrid available or feasible where you are?
7. What is your energy-sovereignty goal for the next 1–3 years?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Sol** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **EAR**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#sol-energy`.
4. Raven folds your EAR into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Sol will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
