# 🌲 Cedar — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Cedar** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Cultural Continuity Index (CCI)**.

> **Index:** `CCI: CCI = (traditions_preserved + innovations_adopted) / total_cultural_assets  (high CCI = holds its past while choosing its future)`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. What languages do you know, and which do you actively practice?
2. What ceremonies, traditions, or observances are part of your life?
3. Who are the knowledge-holders (elders, mentors) in your family or community, and what do they carry?
4. Which traditional skills or crafts do you hold or want to learn?
5. What cultural knowledge do you fear could be lost in your lifetime?
6. How connected do you feel to your lineage and community of origin?
7. What one act of cultural transmission would you most like to complete this year?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Cedar** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **CCI**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#cedar-cultural`.
4. Raven folds your CCI into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Cedar will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
