# 🪨 Stone — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Stone** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Local Resource Autonomy (LRA)**.

> **Index:** `LRA: LRA = vital_resources_met_locally / total_vital_resources  (LRA = 1 is full sovereignty; LRA < 0.5 is dangerous dependency)`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. Where does your food come from — roughly what % is grown or produced locally?
2. What is your water source, and is any of it harvested or regenerated locally?
3. Do you own, rent, or otherwise hold your shelter, and how secure is it?
4. How do you access healthcare, and how much is preventive vs. reactive?
5. Which of these four (food, water, shelter, health) feels most fragile if supply chains failed?
6. What local resources (land, water rights, community assets) do you already have access to?
7. What one dependency would you most like to reduce this year?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Stone** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **LRA**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#stone-physical`.
4. Raven folds your LRA into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Stone will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
