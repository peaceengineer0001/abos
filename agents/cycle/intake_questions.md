# 🔄 Cycle — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Cycle** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Circularity Index (CI)**.

> **Index:** `CI: CI = Reused / Discarded`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 3) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. What are your largest waste streams (organic, plastic, metal, electronic, paper)?
2. Do you compost organic waste? What fraction?
3. How much of what you discard could be reused, repaired, or repurposed?
4. Do you repair items or replace them by default?
5. Are there local reuse, recycling, or repair resources available?
6. What single waste stream would you most like to close the loop on?
7. What is your circularity goal?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Cycle** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **CI**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#cycle-waste`.
4. Raven folds your CI into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Cycle will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
