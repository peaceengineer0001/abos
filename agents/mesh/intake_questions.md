# 🕸️ Mesh — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Mesh** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Freedom of Flow Ratio (FFR)**.

> **Index:** `FFR: FFR = unrestricted_info / total_info`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. Which platforms do you rely on for communication and information?
2. How exposed are you to censorship, de-platforming, or outages?
3. Do you use any decentralized or self-hosted communication tools?
4. Where are the single points of failure in your information flow?
5. Are you familiar with Nostr or mesh networking?
6. Could you host or join a self-hosted relay?
7. What is your communication-sovereignty goal?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Mesh** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **FFR**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#mesh-communication`.
4. Raven folds your FFR into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Mesh will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
