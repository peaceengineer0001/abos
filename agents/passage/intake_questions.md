# 🚢 Passage — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Passage** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Mobility Autonomy Fraction (MAF)**.

> **Index:** `MAF: MAF = sustainable_miles / total_miles`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. Roughly how many miles do you travel per month, and by what modes?
2. What fraction of your travel is by clean/sustainable means?
3. What fuel powers your primary transportation?
4. How exposed are you to fuel-price or supply shocks?
5. Is shared or community mobility available where you are?
6. Could you shift trips to walking, cycling, transit, or an EV on local power?
7. What is your mobility-sovereignty goal?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Passage** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **MAF**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#passage-transportation`.
4. Raven folds your MAF into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Passage will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
