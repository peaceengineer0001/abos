# ⚖️ Council — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Council** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Justice Coherence Index (JCI)**.

> **Index:** `JCI: JCI = equity_decisions / total_decisions`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. Which governance systems most affect your daily life?
2. How equitable do their decisions feel — who benefits, who is left out?
3. Have you experienced or witnessed inequitable governance outcomes?
4. Are there restorative-justice or equity mechanisms available to you?
5. Do you have or want a sovereignty charter for your community?
6. Where is equity leaking most in the decisions that affect you?
7. What is your governance-sovereignty goal?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Council** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **JCI**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#council-governance`.
4. Raven folds your JCI into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Council will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
