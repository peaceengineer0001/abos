# 🔥 Ember — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Ember** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Debt Freedom Ratio (DFR)**.

> **Index:** `DFR: DFR = 1 − (Debt / Income)  (DFR = 1 is debt-free; DFR < 0 is net negative)`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 1) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. What is your total monthly income from all sources? (stored locally — never leaves your device by default)
2. List each debt you carry: balance, interest rate, and minimum payment.
3. What are your fixed recurring obligations each month?
4. What is your current monthly savings rate, if any?
5. How much of your income goes to debt service each month?
6. Do you have access to any community finance tools (credit unions, mutual aid, local currencies)?
7. What would debt freedom let you invest in that you can't today?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Ember** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **DFR**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#ember-economic`.
4. Raven folds your DFR into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Ember will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
