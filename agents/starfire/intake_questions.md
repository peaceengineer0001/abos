# ✨ Starfire — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Starfire** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Spiritual Coherence Index (Sc)**.

> **Index:** `Sc: Sc rises as the phase alignment θ_coh of individual purpose narrows around the community's shared mission`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 1) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. In one or two sentences, what is your deepest sense of purpose or calling right now?
2. What spiritual, religious, or philosophical tradition (if any) grounds your life?
3. Which regular practices or rituals do you currently observe (daily, weekly, seasonal)?
4. On a scale of 0–10, how aligned does your daily life feel with your stated purpose?
5. How often do you participate in shared ceremony or community-of-meaning gatherings?
6. Do you use a wearable that can share HRV or coherence data? (optional, private by default)
7. Where do you feel the largest gap between who you want to be and how you actually spend your days?
8. What one coherence practice, if made a habit, would most change your life this year?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Starfire** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **Sc**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#starfire-spiritual`.
4. Raven folds your Sc into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Starfire will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
