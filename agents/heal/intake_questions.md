# ❤️‍🩹 Heal — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Heal** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Wellness Autonomy Index (WAI)**.

> **Index:** `WAI: WAI = (Preventive + Regenerative care) / Total care  (WAI > 0.5 signals a sovereign-health orientation)`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 1) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. How would you split your health spending/time between preventive and reactive care?
2. What preventive practices do you maintain (nutrition, movement, sleep, stress)?
3. How dependent are you on pharmaceuticals for chronic conditions?
4. Do you have access to community or cooperative health resources?
5. How is your nutrition — how much whole, local food do you eat?
6. Do you track any biomarkers (HRV, sleep, labs)?
7. What preventive-health change would most improve your resilience?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Heal** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **WAI**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#heal-health`.
4. Raven folds your WAI into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Heal will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
