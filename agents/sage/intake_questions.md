# 🦉 Sage — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Sage** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Information Flow Efficiency (IFE)**.

> **Index:** `IFE: IFE = knowledge_applied / knowledge_consumed  (IFE near 1 = learning converted to action; near 0 = extractive education)`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. What are the main sources you learn from (books, courses, podcasts, mentors, doing)?
2. In the last month, name one thing you learned and actually applied to a decision or project.
3. Roughly what fraction of what you consume do you put into practice? (0–100%)
4. Which sovereignty skills (energy, water, food, finance, governance) do you most want to build?
5. Are you currently a practitioner or mostly a credential-holder in your field?
6. What knowledge do you keep consuming but never acting on?
7. What is one project that would force you to apply what you've been learning?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Sage** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **IFE**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#sage-mental`.
4. Raven folds your IFE into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Sage will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
