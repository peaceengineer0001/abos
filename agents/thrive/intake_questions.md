# 🌾 Thrive — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Thrive** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Abundance Finance Index (AFI)**.

> **Index:** `AFI: AFI = value_created / debt_issued  (AFI > 1 = operating in abundance; < 1 = leaking value into debt)`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 1) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. How much real value do you or your community create versus credit/debt taken on?
2. Do you use or have access to any local currency or mutual-credit system?
3. Where does value leak out of your local economy?
4. Do you have any shared treasury or mutual-aid mechanism?
5. How much of your spending recirculates locally?
6. Would a community currency or credit pool be feasible?
7. What is your economic-sovereignty goal?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Thrive** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **AFI**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#thrive-economics`.
4. Raven folds your AFI into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Thrive will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
