# ⛰️ Summit — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Summit** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Sovereignty Participation Ratio (SPR)**.

> **Index:** `SPR: SPR = members_actively_self_governing / total_members  (as SPR → 1, political entropy falls and network connectivity λ₂ rises)`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. In which decision-making bodies do you currently participate (councils, co-ops, DAOs, workplace, family)?
2. How much say do you feel you have over the decisions that affect your life?
3. Where is governance in your life most centralized and least accountable?
4. What governance skills (facilitation, budgeting, mediation) do you have or want?
5. Is there a community body you could help start or strengthen?
6. How are decisions currently made in your household or organization?
7. What one step would increase your active participation in self-governance?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Summit** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **SPR**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#summit-political`.
4. Raven folds your SPR into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Summit will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
