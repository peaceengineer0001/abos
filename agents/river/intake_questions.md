# 🌊 River — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **River** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **HRV Coherence (HRVcoh)**.

> **Index:** `HRVcoh: HRVcoh = coherence ratio of the heart-rate-variability spectrum (biomarker of autonomic balance and resilience)`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 1) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. On a typical day, how would you rate your baseline stress from 0 (calm) to 10 (overwhelmed)?
2. Do you have a wearable that can share HRV data? (optional — biometric data stays local by default)
3. What practices, if any, do you use to regulate emotion (breathwork, prayer, exercise, journaling)?
4. How resilient do you feel after a setback — how quickly do you recover?
5. Which relationships give you the most energy, and which drain it?
6. When do you make your worst decisions emotionally, and what triggers that state?
7. What is one small daily practice you'd be willing to try for 30 days?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **River** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **HRVcoh**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#river-emotional`.
4. Raven folds your HRVcoh into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; River will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
