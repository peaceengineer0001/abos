# 🏠 Haven — Guided Intake Questions

These are the onboarding questions **Haven** asks during the **Discover** phase to establish your baseline **Housing Independence Score (HIS)**.

> **Index:** `HIS: HIS = Regen_units / Total_housing`
> **Privacy:** answers are stored locally (privacy tier 2) and never leave your device without explicit consent.

## Baseline Questions

1. Do you own, rent, or otherwise hold your current dwelling?
2. How secure is your housing over the next 5 years?
3. Does your dwelling integrate any of its own energy, water, or waste systems?
4. What materials is it built from — any local or regenerative content?
5. What fraction of your income goes to rent or mortgage?
6. Are off-grid retrofits or modular options feasible for you?
7. What is your shelter-sovereignty goal?

## What Happens With Your Answers

1. **Haven** uses your answers to calculate your baseline **HIS**.
2. It identifies your biggest dependency in this domain (the "fuel barge" — the single highest-leverage point).
3. It designs a scope-appropriate optimization plan and posts it to `#haven-shelter`.
4. Raven folds your HIS into your overall **Pe** and **CVI** scores.

You can update any answer at any time; Haven will recalculate and re-plan automatically.
