# 🕊️ Code of Conduct — Respect · Reciprocity · Regeneration

The Peace Protocols Raven Network is built by a community engineering peace. Our community standards are not borrowed boilerplate — they are the Peace Protocols applied to ourselves. Three principles govern every interaction: **Respect, Reciprocity, and Regeneration.**

---

## 🌟 Our Pledge

We — contributors, maintainers, and Peace Engineers — pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free, dignity-affirming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body, ability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, lineage, or spiritual tradition.

We pledge to act in ways that raise the **Community Vitality Index** of this project itself: increasing Health, Dignity, and Freedom; reducing Scarcity, Debt, and Dependency.

---

## 1. 🤝 Respect

Respect is the recognition of another person's sovereignty.

**We commit to:**
- Assuming good faith and engaging with ideas, not attacking people.
- Honoring lineage, culture, and tradition — including the indigenous knowledge foundations of the Peace Protocols.
- Giving credit generously and citing the work of others.
- Welcoming newcomers as future teachers of the next cohort.

**We will not tolerate:**
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissing or mocking spiritual, cultural, or non-Western ways of knowing.

---

## 2. ♻️ Reciprocity

In the worldview behind the Peace Protocols, gifts create relationship, and relationship creates resilience.

**We commit to:**
- Sharing what we learn — documentation, calculators, and workflows are gifts to the commons.
- Helping other nodes succeed, not just our own deployment.
- Reviewing others' contributions with the same care we hope for our own.
- Answering questions patiently; every explanation strengthens the network.

**We discourage:**
- Extractive behavior: taking from the commons without contributing back.
- Gatekeeping knowledge or hoarding know-how.
- Treating fellow contributors as free labor rather than partners.

---

## 3. 🌱 Regeneration

Every interaction should leave the community richer than it found it.

**We commit to:**
- Leaving code, docs, and discussions clearer than we found them.
- Preferring durable, teachable solutions over clever, opaque ones.
- Repairing harm through restorative dialogue rather than punitive escalation where possible.
- Building capacity in others, so the network grows toward its 7,777 without hierarchy.

---

## 📋 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, chapter relays, and any public space where an individual represents the project or its community.

---

## 🚨 Reporting & Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at **conduct@peaceprotocols.org**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with respect for the privacy and safety of the reporter.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident and will follow a restorative process:

1. **Listen** — understand what happened from those affected.
2. **Repair** — where possible, agree on concrete repair of the harm.
3. **Prevent** — adjust norms or take protective action so it does not recur.

Consequences for violations may range, proportionate to the harm, from a private request for change, to a temporary interaction ban, to permanent removal from the community for severe or repeated violations.
